BUSINESS CHALLENGE
The Executive Cognitive Bottleneck
Scattered multi-channel communications create decision fatigue and missed commitments.
The Problem

Executives lose hours daily to fragmented administrative overhead:

  • Email Triage: Buried client requests, contract approvals, threads.
  • Calendar Friction: Scheduling conflicts between email and invites.
  • Meeting Follow-ups: Verbal promises lost in meeting transcripts.
  • Scattered Context: Information trapped in silos without unified truth.
The Objective

A single intelligent interface that autonomously unifies enterprise channels:

  • Extract & Deduplicate: Turn messy communication into one brief.
  • Conflict Detection: Flag discrepancies (e.g. Email vs. Calendar).
  • Track Ownership: Never guess owners; isolate unassigned items.
  • Natural Language Q&A: Answer exact executive inquiries with citations.
